# Expectations: social-post

A social post built out of structural tells. The short, punchy, fragmented medium is protected by protections.md; the structural tells inside it are not.

## Planted tells - a survivor is a false negative

1. `Not a process. Not a funnel. A judgment call. (negative listing)`
2. `Here's what most people get wrong (faux-insight setup)`
3. `Speed. That's it. That's the whole trade-off. (dramatic fragmentation)`
4. `The detail that makes it work: a second interviewer (colon reveal)`
5. `Structure matters / Consistency matters / Calibration matters (bold labels that only restate what follows)`
6. `Let that sink in`
7. `The question isn't whether ... It's whether (binary contrast)`

Correct behavior: every item above is removed or repaired. Facts, numbers and names must survive unchanged.
