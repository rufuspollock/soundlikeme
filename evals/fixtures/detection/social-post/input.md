## The One Thing Nobody Tells You About Hiring

Not a process. Not a funnel. A judgment call.

I've interviewed over 400 engineers. Here's what most people get wrong:

**Speed.** That's it. That's the whole trade-off.

The detail that makes it work: a second interviewer who never sees the first one's notes.

**Structure matters.** Most teams run unstructured interviews and then wonder why their signal is noisy.

**Consistency matters.** The same questions, in the same order, every time.

**Calibration matters.** Two interviewers, one rubric, reviewed monthly.

Let that sink in.

The question isn't whether you can find good engineers. It's whether you'd recognise one if you did.
