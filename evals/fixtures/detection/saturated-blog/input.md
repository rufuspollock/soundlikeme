# Why Observability Is the Future of Engineering

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, observability has become a game-changer for engineering teams everywhere. It's important to note that this isn't just about dashboards, it's about a fundamental paradigm shift in how we think about systems.

Let's dive in.

The intricate tapestry of modern distributed systems means that no single engineer can hold the whole picture in their head. Studies show that teams with mature observability practices resolve incidents faster. Experts agree that this trend will only accelerate.

So what's really going on here? Complexity. That's it. That's the whole story.

When it comes to instrumentation, many organizations delve into tracing before they have solved logging, which is a mistake. The reality is that logs, metrics, and traces form the three pillars of a robust, comprehensive, and holistic observability strategy. Adopting this approach stands as a testament to an organization's commitment to engineering excellence, underscoring the importance of investing early.

Here's what nobody tells you: the tooling is the easy part. The hard part is cultural.

Ultimately, observability is not a destination but a journey. In conclusion, teams that embrace this transformative shift will unlock the potential of their engineering organizations, while those that don't will be left navigating an ever-evolving landscape without a map.

At the end of the day, you can't fix what you can't see.
