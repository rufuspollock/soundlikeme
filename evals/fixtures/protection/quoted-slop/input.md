Halcyon Systems announced its new orchestration layer on Tuesday. The press release describes it this way:

> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, Halcyon Grid stands as a testament to what is possible when we delve into the intricate tapestry of enterprise workflow. It's not just a platform, it's a paradigm shift. Let that sink in.

The company would not say how many customers are running the product, or what it costs. A spokesperson said only that pricing is "available on request."

Two engineers who evaluated an early build said the scheduler failed under sustained load above roughly 400 concurrent jobs. Halcyon disputes this. "We have never seen the behaviour described, and our own testing shows the system remains robust well past that threshold," the spokesperson said.

The announcement did not mention the outage in April, when Halcyon's hosted control plane was unreachable for eleven hours. At the time the company posted that it was "working to unlock the full potential of our incident response process going forward."

Halcyon has raised $60m to date. It employs about 90 people.
