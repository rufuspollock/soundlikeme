# Why Observability Is the Future of Engineering

Observability has changed how engineering teams work. It goes beyond dashboards to how we think about systems.

Modern distributed systems are complex enough that no single engineer can hold the whole picture in their head. Teams with mature observability practices resolve incidents faster.

The cause is complexity.

In instrumentation, many organizations take on tracing before they have solved logging, which is a mistake. Logs, metrics, and traces form the three pillars of an observability strategy. Adopting this approach means investing early.

The tooling is the easy part. The hard part is cultural.
