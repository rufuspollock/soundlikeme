# Sound Like Me

Make AI-generated writing sound like *you* -- or your team, or your organisation.

## The Problem

AI writing sounds like AI. When you dictate ideas, brainstorm, or draft with an AI assistant, the output loses your voice. It comes out generic and flat. You want two things at once: writing that sounds like you *and* writing that's actually good -- concise, clear, well-structured.

## What's In This Repo

Two agent skills built to the [agentskills.io](https://agentskills.io) specification, plus the research behind them.

### `skills/voice-extractor`

Point it at 2-5 samples of your writing. It generates a compact voice profile (<500 words) covering your tone, sentence structure, vocabulary, signature moves, and what you *don't* do. Cheap on tokens, reusable across sessions.

### `skills/write-like-me`

Load this on demand when you want polished output. It applies three layers:

1. **Your voice profile** -- generated by `voice-extractor` or written manually
2. **Conciseness rules** -- 12 distilled Strunk & White principles with examples (optionally backed by the [full Elements of Style](https://github.com/obra/the-elements-of-style))
3. **AI anti-pattern banlist** -- specific words, phrases, and structural tics that make writing sound like AI ("delve", "landscape", the dreaded snappy triad)

The idea: draft freely, then invoke this at the polish step.

### `docs/research.md`

A survey of techniques for voice-matching with AI: few-shot examples, extracted style descriptions, adjective-based prompting, anti-patterns, and existing tools. Includes sources.

## Install

```bash
npx skills add rufuspollock/soundlikeme/skills/voice-extractor
npx skills add rufuspollock/soundlikeme/skills/write-like-me
```

Works with Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and [other agents that support the agentskills.io spec](https://skills.sh/).

## Usage

1. **Generate a voice profile:** Run `voice-extractor` with a few samples of your writing. It saves a profile to `write-like-me/references/voice-profile.md`.
2. **Write or dictate freely** in your normal workflow.
3. **Polish:** Load `write-like-me` when you want the output to sound like you. It rewrites using your profile, conciseness rules, and anti-pattern bans.

## Who It's For

- Individuals who write with AI and want to keep their voice
- Organisations that need consistent tone across AI-assisted content
- Anyone who wants AI output that reads like a human wrote it -- a *specific* human

## Status

Early and experimental. The skills work but need more testing across different types of writing.
