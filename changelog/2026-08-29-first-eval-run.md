The harness went from written to executed. One case: a 2011 post of Rufus's that the voice profile was not built from, stripped to a bulleted brief, drafted back from that brief, and scored against the real thing by three judges who saw neither the profile nor each other's scores nor which text was which.

Ceiling — a different real Pollock piece, scored the same way — 34 out of 35. The profile-on draft, 31. The no-profile control, 24. The profile won on all seven dimensions, which is the answer to the question the ablation exists to ask: it is not decoration.

The more useful result is what it lost points for. Two judges independently docked the profile-on draft for being *more polished and more evenly paced* than Rufus actually is — his crowded parentheticals smoothed into tidier clauses, his sentence-length sprawl trimmed. The profile names both habits, the skill's own guidance already says "flat is a tell too", and both were loaded when the draft was written. Regression toward clean prose looks like a standing force rather than a missing rule, which is a more interesting problem than a banlist gap.

Also: four of the seven dimensions turned out to have a one-point range between floor and ceiling, so their scores are noise rather than signal. And the run found three bugs in its own harness — a judge credited the control for British spelling the reference doesn't use, and another spotted a fabricated fact in the control, wrote it down, and scored around it. Two are fixed.
